# Glossary

### Add-on
An optional modification or extra ingredient that a customer can attach to a menu item during ordering, such as extra sauce, poached egg, or a takeaway box.

### Customer
An individual browsing the menu, placing an order, or redeeming loyalty rewards through the customer web application.

### Fulfillment Status
The state of an order in the kitchen workflow. Values are received, preparing, ready, fulfilled, or archived.

### Guest
A customer who places an order without creating or logging into a member account. Guests do not earn loyalty stamps or redeem rewards.

### Loyalty Card
A virtual twelve-stamp reward tracker attached to a member profile. Every purchased main grants one stamp. Milestone circles at four, eight, and twelve stamps unlock food rewards.

### Loyalty Stamp
A circular visual badge marked onto a member's loyalty card, representing an earned point from purchasing a main dish or credited by staff.

### Main
A primary pasta or breakfast dish on the menu. Purchasing a main entitles a member to earn one loyalty stamp.

### Member
A customer who has authenticated with email and password, phone number, or Google authentication, enabling loyalty tracking and order history.

### Member Migration QR
A QR code presented by a member in the customer application that encodes their unique member identifier, allowing admin staff to scan and transfer stamps from an existing physical card.

### Operating Schedule
The weekly recurring time windows during which the kitchen accepts customer orders.

### Payment Method
The channel through which a customer settles payment for an order. Supported options are Credit Card, PayNow QR, and Pay at Counter.

### Payment Status
The financial settlement state of an order. Marked as paid once funds are confirmed, or unpaid when pending settlement at the counter.

### Redeemed Milestone
A visual badge marked over a reward milestone circle on a loyalty card, indicating the customer has claimed the reward for that milestone.

### Shop Status
The operational state of the store, either open or closed for the day. Can be set automatically by the operating schedule or manually overridden by admin staff.

### Side
A secondary food item on the menu, such as fried chicken, soups, bread, or fries.
